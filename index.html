Our story

Made the way home food should be

Usha’s Products began the way most good kitchens do — with jars handed to neighbours and relatives who kept asking for more. Every pickle is cut, salted and matured by hand. Every spice is dried and ground without fillers or colour. Nothing is made in bulk and left to sit.

What you receive is what we would put on our own table: honest ingredients, traditional Kerala recipes, and a jar that tastes like it came from a home rather than a factory.

  • Prepared in a home kitchen, in small batches
  • No preservatives, artificial colours or flavours
  • Sealed and dispatched only after your order
  • Bulk and gift orders welcome
